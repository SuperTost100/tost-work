The alpha welcome, and a way to report problems

The backgrounds are screenshots of the real app; the cards on top are the proposal. Every button works: "Segnala un problema" opens the report card, and its buttons say what would happen. Pick A or B for the welcome.

A · A note from you

A small card, like a handwritten note: an amber "alpha" stamp, two short paragraphs and your signature. It appears once, the first time someone reaches Home in an alpha build.

Report a problem, from Settings

A new item under Novità and Informazioni (and the same in the Help menu). It opens this card. "Apri GitHub" opens the bug form already filled with the version and the system; the person writes what happened. Logs stay optional and are exported to a file they can check first.

Segnala un problema

Si apre il modulo per i problemi su GitHub, già compilato con questi dati. Tu scrivi cosa è successo e, se puoi, come farlo succedere di nuovo.
