# Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Tost Work collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and its author has no server that receives your data. This notice explains what stays on your computer and what goes to the AI services you choose. The Italian version is the one that counts; this English version is a translation.

## What stays on your computer

Tost Work keeps these in its data folder:

- your settings, projects, tasks and their history, and jobs;
- what you write in "About you";
- the copies of files a task changed, which let you restore the original;
- the app's log, and, only while you turn on "Show technical details and debug", a full log of each task that includes the text of your files and requests;
- your API key, encrypted with your system's secure storage. If your system offers none, the key is kept only until you close the app.

The skills you make are in `Documents/Tost Work/Skills`, and the files of your projects stay in the folders where you keep them.

The data folder is `~/Library/Application Support/Tost Work` on macOS, `%APPDATA%\Tost Work` on Windows and `~/.config/Tost Work` on Linux. Deleting the app, this folder and `Documents/Tost Work` removes everything Tost Work stored.

## What goes to OpenAI or Anthropic

Tasks run in the Codex CLI by OpenAI or in Claude Code by Anthropic, signed in to your account. While a task runs, they send OpenAI or Anthropic:

- your request, your answers to the AI's questions and the files you attach;
- the files the AI reads in the project folder;
- what you wrote in "About you", which goes with every task;
- the web searches the AI makes.

The Codex CLI and Claude Code keep your sign-in on your computer, in Tost Work's data folder. They may also send OpenAI and Anthropic data about how they're used, as they do outside Tost Work.

OpenAI and Anthropic handle all of this under their own privacy policies and your account's settings, including whether your conversations may be used to train their models:

- [OpenAI privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [Anthropic privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)

To see, export or delete what they hold, or to change your training settings, use your OpenAI or Anthropic account.

## When you share something yourself

- **Feedback:** "Send feedback" opens a new issue on GitHub in your browser, with the app's version, your system and your language filled in. You decide what to add before you post it. Issues on GitHub are public, and GitHub's privacy policy applies.
- **Exported logs:** "Export logs" makes a zip on your computer with the app's logs and a summary of your settings, with keys hidden and without your name or your accounts. If debug was on, the logs include the text of your files and requests. The zip goes to someone only if you send it.
- **Email:** if you write to Tommaso Barbera, he uses your address and your message only to answer you, and deletes them when they're no longer needed.

## Who is responsible

Tost Work's author receives no personal data from the app, and what it keeps on your computer is yours alone. For the data sent to OpenAI and Anthropic, they are responsible under their policies. For emails you send him, the controller is Tommaso Barbera, and you can ask him to see or delete them at the address below. You can also complain to your data protection authority, which in Italy is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

## Changes

This notice may change with a new version of Tost Work. The new version comes with the app and shows its date at the top.
